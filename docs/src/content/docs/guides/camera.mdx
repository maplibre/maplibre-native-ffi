This guide shows how to read and change a map's camera, from your code and from
gestures. A [](api:mln_camera) has a center, a zoom, a bearing, and a pitch.

## Read the camera

Read the camera from the map's snapshot with [](api:mln_map_get_snapshot). To
read it after your pending changes apply, use [](api:mln_map_get_camera)
instead.

<Snippet page="camera" region="read" />

## Move the camera

To move the camera, send a camera update with [](api:mln_map_update_camera) in
jump mode. Set only the fields you want to change.

<Snippet page="camera" region="jump" />

## Animate the camera

To animate, send the update in ease or fly mode with a duration. Ease moves
straight to the target, while fly zooms out, travels, and zooms back in.

To act when an animation ends, set a [](api:mln_camera_transition_handler).

To stop an animation where it is, cancel the map's camera transitions with
[](api:mln_map_cancel_camera_transitions).

<Snippet page="camera" region="animate" />

## Fit the camera

To show an area, query the camera that fits its bounding box with
[](api:mln_map_camera_for_lat_lng_bounds), then jump or animate to that camera.
For a list of coordinates or a GeoJSON geometry, use
[](api:mln_map_camera_for_lat_lngs) or [](api:mln_map_camera_for_geometry)
instead.

<Snippet page="camera" region="fit" />

## Limit the camera

To keep the camera within an area, set the map's bounds with
[](api:mln_map_set_bounds). Bounds limit the center to an area, and the zoom and
pitch to ranges.

<Snippet page="camera" region="limit" />

## Apply gestures

The library doesn't handle input, so your app recognizes gestures from its own
input events and applies them to the camera as deltas. Screen points are in
logical pixels from the map's top-left corner.

When a gesture starts, send a delta with [](api:mln_map_apply_camera_delta) in
the begin phase, which stops running camera animations. For each movement, send
a delta in the update phase with the change since the last one: the movement as
the offset for a drag, or the scale and the pinch's center as the anchor for a
pinch. When the gesture ends, send the end phase, or the cancel phase if the
platform cancels it.

<Snippet page="camera" region="gesture" />

## Convert coordinates

A [](api:mln_map_projection) converts between screen points and coordinates for
one view of the map. Create it from the snapshot's view, and create a new one
after the camera moves. Close it when you're done.

<Snippet page="camera" region="convert" />
